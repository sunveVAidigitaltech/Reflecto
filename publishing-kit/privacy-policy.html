Privacy Policy

Application: Reflecto: Light Laser Puzzle

Developer: Sunve VAI Digital Tech

Effective Date: October 14, 2026 | Last Updated: October 14, 2026

Designed for Families & COPPA Compliant

1. Introduction

Sunve VAI Digital Tech ("we", "us", or "our") is dedicated to protecting the privacy of all players, with particular care for children and families. This Privacy Policy details our data practices for the Android mobile application Reflecto: Light Laser Puzzle.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

Reflecto does not require account registration, passwords, or personal profile creation. We do not gather, store, or share personally identifiable information, including names, home addresses, phone numbers, email addresses, or device location coordinates.

All gameplay data (such as puzzle completion, star counts, unlocked chapters, and audio preferences) is stored locally on your device via standard local storage. This data is never transmitted to our servers.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

To keep Reflecto free for all players, the App displays advertisements provided by Google AdMob (Google LLC).

Google AdMob may collect and process anonymous technical identifiers, such as:

  • Google Advertising ID (AAID) for advertising and fraud prevention
  • Approximate geographical location at the city/country level (derived from IP address)
  • Ad interaction metrics (impressions, clicks, and rewarded video completions)
  • Technical diagnostic data (crash logs and performance indicators)

For detailed information on how Google manages advertising information, please review the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

4. Children’s Privacy & COPPA Compliance

Reflecto strictly complies with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the Google Play Families Policy:

  • Family-Safe Ads Only: All served advertisements are strictly filtered to child-appropriate, family-safe categories (Rating G / Everyone).
  • No Behavioral Tracking: We configure the Google Mobile Ads SDK with child-directed treatment flags to disable interest-based behavioral profiling.
  • No In-App Purchases: Reflecto contains no microtransactions, digital store purchases, or paywalls.
  • No Social Links in Gameplay: The game experience is self-contained without social media integrations or external chat features.

5. Device Permissions

Reflecto requests only the minimal permissions required for operation:

  • android.permission.INTERNET: Enables loading AdMob advertisements and web assets.
  • android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Allows checking network connectivity before requesting ad content.
  • android.permission.VIBRATE: Provides tactile haptic feedback during mirror rotations and puzzle victories.

6. Data Control and Deletion

Because no player data is transmitted to external servers, you have full control over your local data. To erase all game progress, simply clear the app's cache and storage via Android Settings > Apps > Reflecto > Storage & cache > Clear storage, or uninstall the app.

7. Policy Updates

We may periodically update this policy to reflect changes in legal requirements or app features. Updates will appear on this page with a revised "Last Updated" timestamp.
