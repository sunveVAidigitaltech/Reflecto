## 1. Introduction

Sunve VAI Digital Tech is committed to protecting the privacy of our users, especially children and families. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you download, install, and play **Reflecto: Light Laser Puzzle** on Android devices.

By downloading or using Reflecto, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

## 2. Information We Do NOT Collect

Reflecto does **not** require user accounts, logins, or registration. We do **not** collect or store personal identifying information such as:
- Your name, address, or email address
- Your phone number
- Your physical location / GPS coordinates
- Your device contacts, photos, or microphone recordings

All puzzle gameplay, stars earned, unlocked levels, and audio/theme preferences are stored **locally on your device** using standard browser/WebView local storage (`localStorage`). This data never leaves your device.

## 3. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

Reflecto displays advertisements through **Google AdMob** (a service provided by Google LLC) to support free access to the game.

### How AdMob Handles Data
Google AdMob may collect and process certain non-personally identifiable technical information to serve ads, prevent fraud, and measure ad performance, including:
- Device identifiers (such as the Google Advertising ID / AAID)
- IP address (used for approximate geographic location at city/country level)
- Interaction data (ad impressions, clicks, video views)
- Diagnostic information (app performance, crash logs)

For more information on how Google processes and protects advertising data, please visit:
- **Google Privacy Policy:** [https://policies.google.com/privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

## 4. Children's Privacy & COPPA Compliance

We take children's privacy seriously and strictly adhere to:
- The **Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA)**
- The **Google Play Families Policy**

### Family-Safe Advertising Guarantee:
1. **Ad Filtering**: All advertisements served within Reflecto are restricted to family-safe and child-appropriate categories (content rating G / Everyone).
2. **No Behavioral Profiling**: We configure Google Mobile Ads with the `TAG_FOR_CHILD_DIRECTED_TREATMENT` parameter where applicable, ensuring personalized tracking and behavioral advertising are disabled for child audiences.
3. **No In-App Purchases (IAP)**: There are no microtransactions or paywalls within Reflecto, preventing accidental charges.
4. **No External Links in Child Flows**: Core gameplay is self-contained and free of external links or social media integrations.

If a parent or guardian believes their child has provided personal information without consent, please contact us immediately, and we will delete such data promptly.

---

## 5. Device Permissions Used

Reflecto requests only the minimal system permissions necessary for core game features:
- `android.permission.INTERNET`: Required to fetch and display AdMob ads and allow web assets to function.
- `android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE`: Required by AdMob to verify whether the device has an active internet connection before requesting ads.
- `android.permission.VIBRATE`: Used solely for subtle tactile haptic feedback when rotating mirrors or solving chambers.

Reflecto does **not** request camera, microphone, storage, contacts, or location permissions.

---

## 6. Data Retention & Deletion

Since we do not operate external user database servers, we do not store your gameplay data on our servers. 

If you wish to clear all game progress, hints, and local settings, you can do so at any time by:
1. Going to Android **Settings** > **Apps** > **Reflecto**.
2. Selecting **Storage & cache** > **Clear storage** (or uninstalling the app).
